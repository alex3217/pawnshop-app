## Authoritative decisions

- Money is calculated in integer cents. `MarketplaceTransaction.subtotal` is the completed-sale value; taxes, shipping, fees, settlement, payout, refund and ledger rows are not added to it.
- A completed sale is one distinct `MarketplaceTransaction.id` whose status is `COMPLETED`. `completedAt` is freshness and days-to-sale evidence. This prevents duplicate counting across transaction, settlement, and payout systems.
- Active marketplace supply is `MarketplaceListing.status = ACTIVE`, with a positive price, active seller, non-deleted shop when present, and a non-deleted/available linked item when present.
- Legacy buyer item detail remains the public target source. Comparable marketplace rows are matched by normalized category, deterministic title compatibility, condition preference, price proximity and state preference.
- No authoritative price-history or view/search-event source exists. V1 reports these as unavailable and creates no schema or migration.
- Demand uses only aggregate saved searches, watchlists, offers, completed sales and buyer submissions where the audience is authorized. Signal weights and thresholds are centralized and deterministic.
- Owner intelligence remains behind the existing `growth:read` shop authorization and seller entitlement service. Super Admin intelligence remains behind the existing server-side `SUPER_ADMIN` router guard.

## Audit result

V1 can be implemented without a schema change or migration. The implementation will add deterministic utilities and aggregation services, extend the existing Business Growth response, and add public item and protected Super Admin endpoints. Historical pricing, event-based search/view analytics, structured brand/model fields, forecasts, anonymous benchmarking, exports and all generative AI remain deferred.
