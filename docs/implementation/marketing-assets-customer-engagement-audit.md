## Schema decision

The audit found no reusable shop-follow or referral-attribution relation. The smallest nondestructive addition is therefore: `ShopFollow`, `ReferralCode`, and `ReferralAttribution`, with relations to existing `User` and `PawnShop`. No campaign, notification, buyer, shop, subscription, entitlement, analytics, reward, or contact model is duplicated. A SQL migration will be authored but not applied.

## Delivery boundary

V1 generates assets on demand and delivers shop marketing notifications in-app only. Email, SMS, real bulk sends, rewards, arbitrary templates, external redirects, automated lifecycle-wide event fan-out, stored binaries, and private follower lists are outside this phase.
