# Legal and Business Operating Procedures

Status: **DRAFT — PROCEDURES REQUIRE COUNSEL AND OWNER APPROVAL PLUS REHEARSAL EVIDENCE**

These procedures define a minimum operating framework. Each procedure must be tailored to the approved geography and product scope. A checklist is not operational evidence until named personnel successfully rehearse it using approved synthetic or authorized cases.

## Common control requirements

Every case must have:

- unique case ID and type;
- intake timestamp and source;
- authenticated requester/subject/shop identity where required;
- assigned owner and alternate;
- severity and response target;
- applicable jurisdiction, document version and transaction/listing identifiers;
- preservation/legal-hold status;
- actions, approvals and immutable audit references;
- communications record without unnecessary sensitive data;
- resolution, financial/data reconciliation and closure approval; and
- post-incident or trend follow-up where required.

Secrets, full payment credentials, raw MFA values, government IDs, unredacted legal demands and unnecessary personal data must not be copied into ordinary tickets or repository files.

## SOP-01: Pawn-shop eligibility and onboarding

**Trigger:** A shop applies to participate or its eligibility materially changes.

1. Collect only counsel-approved identity, business-authority, license, location, beneficial-owner and contact evidence.
2. Verify evidence using the approved source and record verifier, timestamp, jurisdiction, expiration and result.
3. Screen against approved prohibited/sanctions/fraud criteria where legally required and operationally authorized.
4. Confirm signed shop agreement and current policy versions.
5. Configure only the approved plan, roles, transaction categories, geography and caps.
6. Complete role/security/MFA and prohibited-item training.
7. Perform a bounded onboarding rehearsal without live customer funds.
8. Obtain Business, Compliance/Legal, Security and Support entry approvals.
9. Reverify on expiration, ownership change, incident, material risk signal or approved cadence.

**Stop/escalate:** unverifiable authority/license, prohibited jurisdiction/category, sanctions/fraud concern, expired agreement, failed security controls, or missing owner approval.

## SOP-02: Prohibited, stolen, counterfeit, recalled, or hazardous item response

1. Disable or quarantine the listing/item without destroying evidence.
2. Prevent sale, auction, payment, payout, transfer and republication as approved.
3. Preserve listing, media, actor, shop, transaction, message and audit references.
4. Route the case to Trust & Safety and Legal/Compliance; notify Security if account compromise is suspected.
5. Apply counsel-approved customer/shop notice, law-enforcement cooperation and disclosure rules.
6. Record appeal and review rights where applicable.
7. Release, return, retain, report or dispose only under approved authority.
8. Reconcile affected transactions, refunds, disputes and payouts.

**Never:** promise law-enforcement action, reveal reporter identity, delete preservation evidence, or return suspected property without approved authority.

## SOP-03: Refund, return, cancellation, dispute, and chargeback

1. Authenticate the requester and identify transaction, processor event, fulfillment and policy version.
2. Determine eligibility using the approved policy; record any exception and approver.
3. Preserve communications, item condition, shipment/pickup, authorization and processor evidence.
4. Apply payout holds only within approved authority.
5. Execute processor action through least-privilege access with required MFA/dual approval.
6. Reconcile internal settlement, fee, refund, payout and processor identities.
7. Notify affected parties using approved language and escalation channels.
8. Close only with zero unexplained financial variance or documented authorized treatment.

## SOP-04: Daily payment and payout reconciliation

1. Reconcile orders/settlements, payment intents/charges, refunds, disputes, fees, transfers and payouts by immutable provider/internal IDs.
2. Confirm opening balance + activity = closing balance for each approved ledger boundary.
3. Record missing, duplicate, late, reversed and amount/currency mismatches.
4. Assign every variance; unexplained variance blocks expansion and may trigger transaction suspension.
5. Obtain Finance approval for corrections, write-offs, reserves and manual adjustments.
6. Retain a redacted daily evidence record and exception log.

## SOP-05: Privacy request and appeal

1. Accept requests only through approved channels.
2. Record jurisdiction, request type and statutory/business deadline without inferring applicability.
3. Verify identity using a proportionate approved method; avoid collecting unnecessary new data.
4. Search the approved system/vendor inventory.
5. Apply legal holds, security/fraud and retention exceptions under counsel-approved rules.
6. Complete access/correction/deletion/portability/opt-out actions and vendor propagation.
7. Perform second-person quality review.
8. Deliver securely; record completion evidence and appeal instructions.
9. Escalate complaints, appeals and disputed exceptions to Privacy/Legal.

## SOP-06: Security incident and suspected data breach

1. Activate the incident commander and preserve evidence.
2. Contain access, secrets, providers and affected workflows without destroying evidence.
3. Determine affected systems, data, subjects, jurisdictions and time window.
4. Engage Security, Privacy, Counsel, executive, insurer, providers and communications owners.
5. Counsel records notification decisions and deadlines; engineering does not infer them.
6. Communicate only approved, accurate information.
7. Restore from verified clean state; rotate/revoke credentials and validate containment.
8. Complete regulatory/customer/provider notices when authorized.
9. Record timeline, decisions, evidence, reconciliation and corrective actions.

## SOP-07: Legal, regulator, and law-enforcement requests

1. Route all requests to the restricted approved intake.
2. Preserve the original request and delivery metadata.
3. Counsel validates authority, scope, jurisdiction, deadline, confidentiality and notice restrictions.
4. Issue a bounded preservation hold if authorized.
5. Collect only approved responsive data with chain-of-custody evidence.
6. Conduct legal and privacy review before disclosure.
7. Transmit through the approved secure channel and record exactly what was produced.
8. Release holds only under counsel authority.

## SOP-08: Content, messaging, moderation, and IP reports

1. Authenticate the report as appropriate and classify safety, harassment, fraud, illegal content, IP or emergency risk.
2. Restrict privileged message-content access to approved personnel with MFA and audit logging.
3. Preserve relevant evidence while minimizing unrelated private content.
4. Apply the approved moderation action and notify parties when permitted.
5. Provide appeal/review where policy requires.
6. Route DMCA/counter-notice, trademark and repeat-infringer matters through counsel-approved procedures.
7. Escalate credible imminent-harm or illegal-activity concerns through the approved emergency process.

## SOP-09: Subscription cancellation and automatic-renewal support

1. Verify account and applicable plan/document version.
2. Provide the approved cancellation mechanism without unnecessary friction.
3. Display effective date, remaining access, future charges, refund decision and confirmation.
4. Record request, cancellation result and confirmation delivery.
5. Reconcile provider and internal subscription state.
6. Escalate disputed charges, failed cancellation or disclosure concerns to Finance/Legal.

## SOP-10: Vendor onboarding, review, outage, and exit

1. Record purpose, owner, data, access, region, subprocessors, retention, criticality and alternatives.
2. Complete security, privacy, legal, finance and reliability review.
3. Execute required contract/DPA and least-privilege configuration.
4. Verify logs, MFA, backup, deletion, incident contacts and billing/support ownership.
5. Exercise outage and exit procedures for critical vendors.
6. Review on approved cadence and material changes.
7. On exit, revoke access, export required records, verify deletion/retention obligations and update the data map.

## SOP-11: Accessibility request and remediation

1. Provide an accurate public accessibility contact and accommodation route.
2. Record affected workflow, assistive technology, browser/device, severity and requested accommodation.
3. Offer a safe alternative path where available without requiring surrender of rights.
4. Assign remediation owner and test with the affected modality.
5. Communicate status using approved language.
6. Retest, document resolution and add regression coverage where appropriate.

## SOP-12: Beta suspension, shop offboarding, and reactivation

1. Identify the approved trigger: legal/licensing issue, security event, prohibited item, fraud, financial variance, support failure, policy breach, provider risk, or stop threshold.
2. Obtain the required Legal/Compliance, Business, Security and Finance approvals, except approved emergency containment.
3. Suspend only the necessary accounts, listings, transactions, payouts, integrations or writes.
4. Preserve evidence and reconcile open orders, refunds, disputes, subscriptions and payouts.
5. Notify affected parties with approved instructions and appeal/review process.
6. Revoke privileged/vendor access and rotate credentials where appropriate.
7. Reactivate only after every condition is cleared and recorded by the authorized owners.

## SOP-13: Policy publication, assent, amendment, and re-consent

1. Record approved document ID, exact version/SHA, scope, effective date and superseded version.
2. Publish only after counsel/product approval and QA verification.
3. Link the document at every required contextual decision point.
4. Capture affirmative assent and approved evidence fields.
5. Trigger re-consent only according to the approved amendment rules.
6. Preserve historical versions and assent records.
7. Roll back an incorrect publication without deleting evidence; notify affected users if required.

## SOP-14: FFL verification and reverification

**Trigger:** A pawnshop requests firearm controlled-test participation, a license approaches expiration, or ownership/location/license facts materially change.

1. Restrict eligibility to a pawnshop holding a Type 01 or Type 02 FFL and the counsel-approved Texas/local authority.
2. Verify the FFL through the approved authoritative source; record only license number or approved minimized identifier, type, premises state, expiration, verifier, source, UTC time and result.
3. Confirm the licensed premises and participating PawnLoop shop identity match under the counsel-approved rule; escalate rather than infer a match.
4. Confirm business authority, current shop agreement, firearm-policy acknowledgement, required training, support contact and suspension/offboarding contact.
5. Assign the firearm-controlled-test capability only after second-person Compliance/Operations approval and all counsel-required evidence IDs resolve.
6. Reverify before expiration and on ownership, premises, license, disciplinary, incident, provider-policy or material legal change.
7. Remove the capability immediately when verification expires, fails, becomes inconsistent or cannot be reproduced.

**Never:** approve an FFL type other than 01 or 02, accept a customer as the firearm seller, store unnecessary license-holder personal data, or treat a PawnLoop shop account as proof of licensure.

## SOP-15: Firearm listing and reservation review

1. Confirm the shop has a current approved FFL verification and remains in the Texas controlled-test cohort.
2. Confirm the item is an ordinary firearm within the counsel-approved test scope; reject ammunition, suppressors, NFA firearms, destructive devices, and any other specially regulated or deferred item.
3. Review title, description, images, serial-number handling, condition, location, fulfillment language and required public disclosures under the approved policy. Do not expose a full serial number if counsel/security require minimization.
4. Confirm the listing offers only approved public browsing, reservation and shop-contact behavior; no PawnLoop firearm checkout, Stripe payment, direct-to-consumer shipping or customer-to-customer path may be available.
5. Confirm the buyer-facing path requires the approved 21+ attestation and Texas transaction boundary without representing either as the FFL's final eligibility decision.
6. Require second-person review before publication and record reviewer, policy version, listing ID, shop/FFL evidence ID, UTC time and result.
7. Re-review after any material listing edit, shop/FFL status change, complaint, legal change or provider-policy change.

**Stop/escalate:** unverified/expired FFL, non-Texas transaction, buyer under 21, prohibited/deferred subtype, ambiguous transfer method, payment capability, direct shipment, customer seller, misleading eligibility claim, missing disclosure or suspected unlawful/stolen item.

## SOP-16: Firearm transfer-completion attestation

1. Identify the listing/reservation, buyer account, participating shop and selling or receiving FFL using approved minimized references.
2. Require the FFL's authorized actor to attest that identification and every required Form 4473, NICS, acquisition/disposition and reporting step were handled by the FFL before physical release.
3. Record only the approved minimum: FFL verification reference, buyer 21+ attestation, transfer completed/not completed status, transfer-through-FFL attestation, actor role, UTC time, policy version and exception/suspension status.
4. Do not collect or retain Form 4473, NICS records or transaction numbers, government-ID images, background-check answers, denial reasons, or FFL acquisition/disposition records.
5. If transfer does not occur, use only the counsel-approved neutral status; do not record a protected or sensitive reason.
6. Apply second-person exception review and retention/deletion rules approved by Counsel and Privacy.
7. Close the reservation only after audit completeness; reconcile no PawnLoop firearm payment because that capability remains disabled.

## SOP-17: Firearm incident, suspension, and reactivation

1. Immediately quarantine affected listings and suspend firearm reservations/contact capability when an FFL expires or cannot be verified, a prohibited subtype or transfer/shipping bypass appears, a customer seller is detected, payment is attempted through PawnLoop, records exceed the approved data boundary, or a legal/safety/provider concern arises.
2. Preserve minimized listing, reservation, shop, actor, policy, audit and approval references without copying Form 4473, NICS, identification, denial, or other FFL-controlled records.
3. Assign Trust & Safety and Compliance; engage Counsel, Security, Privacy, Finance, the incident commander, or law enforcement only under their approved authority and procedure.
4. Block publication, reservation, payment, platform fees, transfer claims, and reactivation as applicable. Do not interfere with the FFL's independent legal recordkeeping or reporting duties.
5. Record affected scope, decision owner, UTC times, notices, appeal/review, required reverification and corrective actions.
6. Reconcile and delete any improperly collected data only under approved preservation, incident and legal-hold direction.
7. Reactivate only after current FFL reverification, root-cause remediation, counsel/operations approval, security/privacy clearance where applicable, and repeat listing review.

**Immediate beta stop:** evidence of direct-to-consumer shipping, customer-to-customer transfer, firearm payment through unapproved Stripe/PawnLoop paths, systematic age/geography bypass, unlawful transfer facilitation, material prohibited-category exposure, or collection of Form 4473/NICS records.
